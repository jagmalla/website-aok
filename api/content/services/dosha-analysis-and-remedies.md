## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### How I verify a dosha
A dosha is not a label to be read from a single placement. Following **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** and **Phaladeepika**, I weigh the afflicted planet's sign, dignity, house lordship and aspects, the strength of the house it affects, and above all whether a benefic (Jupiter especially) protects it. I check every dosha from the Lagna, the Moon and, for relationship doshas, Venus. Then I ask the most important question: will the dosha actually be activated by your dasha and transits, and when?

### The doshas I examine
- **Mangal (Kuja) Dosha:** Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house, with its many classical cancellations, such as Mars in its own sign, exalted, or aspected by Jupiter.
- **Kaal Sarp Yoga:** all seven planets hemmed between Rahu and Ketu. It is a later tradition rather than a Parashari classic, so I always judge it alongside the full chart and never in isolation.
- **Pitra Dosha:** afflictions to the Sun, the 9th house of ancestors and dharma, and its lord, often involving Rahu or Saturn.
- **Shani-related periods:** Sade Sati (Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st and 2nd from your Moon) and Ashtama Shani (Saturn in the 8th from the Moon). These are temporary phases, not permanent doshas.
- **Grahan (eclipse) and Guru Chandal yogas:** Rahu or Ketu with the Sun, Moon or Jupiter.

### Timing and Nadi insight
The **Vimshottari dasha** shows when an afflicted planet gets to speak. **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** adds which life area a planetary link actually touches, and Jupiter's transit over the afflicted planet often marks the turning point toward relief.

### The remedies I recommend
Remedies in Jyotish are a way of aligning your **Kriyamana karma**, the actions you take now, with the lessons your chart describes. I recommend:

- **Mantra:** the planet's Beej mantra or a classical stotra, chanted with understanding.
- **Dhyana (meditation):** especially for Moon, Rahu and Ketu afflictions that disturb the mind.
- **Dana (charity):** given on the planet's day to people who genuinely need it.
- **Conduct and seva:** service, honesty and discipline in the area the planet governs.
- **Pancha Mahabhuta balance:** simple elemental practices, such as fire for a weak Sun or Mars and water for the Moon, matched to your chart.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a dosha be completely removed?
A dosha describes a karmic pattern in your chart, so it is not erased like a mistake. Its effects can be greatly softened through cancellations already present in the chart, wise timing and sincere remedies. In practice, many people find the dosha simply stops being a source of fear once they understand it.

### Are expensive pujas necessary for dosha remedies?
No. Classical texts emphasise mantra, charity, discipline and devotion, which are free or inexpensive and done by you personally. I never require costly rituals. If you wish to perform a traditional puja, I can explain its purpose, but the most effective remedy is consistent personal practice.

### Is Kaal Sarp Dosha mentioned in classical texts?
Kaal Sarp is not described in Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra and is a later tradition. I do not ignore it, but I judge it alongside the full chart, the strength of the Lagna lord and the dashas, and I never treat it as a fixed curse on its own.
