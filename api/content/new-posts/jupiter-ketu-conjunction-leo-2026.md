During that very window the ruler of Leo (the Sun/Surya) is transiting through Libra where it is considered to be debilitated (neech). With Saturn (Shani) casting its discerning eye from Pisces and Rahu magnifying the ambitions from Aquarius, these 25 days are a period of major inner awakening and redirection on the material plane.

## Detailed Predictions for All 12 Ascendants (Lagna)
### 1. Aries Ascendant (Mesha Lagna)
You may well have concerns about your children's education and health, with the inevitable result of some unforeseen spending. On the plus side, new and unusual sources of income will come to light. In marriage or in a business partnership be very conscious of avoiding ego trips and little arguments in the early days. A matter of maturity and empathy in dealing with these issues will go a long way to sorting out the old grievances and really cementing the relationship.

### 2. Taurus Ascendant (Vrishabha Lagna)
Domestic peace may see some minor friction, with a real need to attend to your mother's health and her emotional state. Professionally though, the use of modern technology, the digital world and the internet is going to be a real growth opportunity for you. Your rivals and competitors are simply not going to be able to touch you. On the financial front too, long standing debts and health issues will finally start to be cleared.

### 3. Gemini Ascendant (Mithuna Lagna)
Daily effort and actual travelling will go up massively, but your perseverance will be rewarded in proportion. Don't fall into the trap of misunderstanding or creating cold distances with your younger siblings. Be extremely cautious in speculative markets and with high risk investments. Your strategic mind will be absolutely at its best and any initial confusion will be quickly resolved to the point where you're making very profitable decisions.

### 4. Cancer Ascendant (Karka Lagna)
Maintain disciplined speech as the last thing you want is to drive your family members away with your words. Keep the expenditure as low as possible to maintain your liquidity. There may be some initial unease over a minor health issue with your mother or some domestic turmoil, but the second half of this period is very conducive to acquiring land, property, a car or some nice domestic luxuries.

### 5. Leo Ascendant (Simha Lagna)
With the transit activating your 1st house you may have periods of fatigue, lethargy or mental isolation. Don't blow any minor disagreements in the marriage too much. There may be a slight dip in personal self confidence but your resilience and creative gifts will see you through and you'll come out on the other side of it all stronger. Public endeavours, creative writing and digital media will be the areas to get recognition.

### 6. Virgo Ascendant (Kanya Lagna)
Unexpected expenses or a trip of some sort may well come up at short notice. The pending litigation, the legal wrangling and the ongoing disputes will all be resolved in your favour in the end. There may be some minor family disputes early on over money, but the route to getting the funds that have been blocked, the assets of the family that have been locked away, the route to the profitable property gains will all be there very soon.

### 7. Libra Ascendant (Tula Lagna)
Don't place too many expectations on casual friends and acquaintances, as some of them may very quickly go cold. Don't go chasing get rich quick schemes or speculative shortcuts. Any initial dip in energy or in social recognition will be very quickly reversed. Your social authority will grow, your voice will be heard and your leadership will be properly appreciated.

### 8. Scorpio Ascendant (Vrishchika Lagna)
Sudden shifts in the workplace or a failure to achieve career aspirations may lead to a moment of discontent and the home front may be a little bit on edge. That said, people working in the overseas markets, the consultancy side and the digital space are in a position to make a very significant amount of money. The outstanding medical and admin bills will be sorted out and that will bring a real sense of peace.

### 9. Sagittarius Ascendant (Dhanu Lagna)
Any disagreements you may have with your dad or your mentor figures are likely to get worse so it would be sensible to avoid any heated arguments. Fortune may seem to be moving very slowly at first but in the long run you will have the opportunity to build up a network of genuine friends, a trusted network and admin recognition which will lead to massive financial rewards. Blocked receivables will almost certainly come back and that will be the realisation of your long held ambitions.

### 10. Capricorn Ascendant (Makara Lagna)
Health is all about proactive care, particularly your digestive system and dietary balance. Be very careful with your choice of words in any sensitive negotiations. On the career front, the long-standing workplace politics and the various bottlenecks will all go away. The senior people will come to accept your ability and that will in turn open the door to more senior positions, greater responsibilities and greater public profile.

### 11. Aquarius Ascendant (Kumbha Lagna)
A slight emotional distance can sometimes arise in a marriage or a business partnership and that will require patience and understanding from both sides. Keep your mind clear of all unnecessary anxiety and doubt. Simply doing some good (charity), going on a spiritual retreat or consulting with an elder will quickly remove any obstacles and bring about a state of good fortune which will turn the situation to your advantage.

### 12. Pisces Ascendant (Meena Lagna)
Gastric / digestive problems can of course lead to some discomfort and so a light, wholesome diet is required. There may be some minor sleep disturbance or night time restlessness but there will be no major crisis or threat to your reputation. Unforeseen financial windfalls via in-laws, insurance and inheritance from the family are all strongly in favour.
