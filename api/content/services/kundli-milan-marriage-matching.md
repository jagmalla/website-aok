### Is a low Guna score an absolute reason not to marry?
Not necessarily. Guna Milan accounts for only a fraction of marital success. Strong 7th house lords, benefic Jupiter aspects, and favorable Navamsha placements often create happy, enduring marriages despite moderate point scores.

### What if one partner has Mangal Dosha and the other does not?
Classical texts outline numerous cancellation conditions (Parihara), such as Mars being in its own sign, aspected by Jupiter, or balanced by Saturn. We evaluate real planetary strengths rather than inducing fear.

### Can we discuss remedies for pre-existing marital stress?
Yes. Kundli Milan is equally valuable for engaged and married couples seeking to understand behavioral cycles, dasha clashes, and astrological remedies to restore harmony.
