## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Accurate calculation
- **Sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsha:** the standard in Indian Jyotish.
- **Historical time zones and daylight saving:** essential for births in India before standardisation, in war-time Britain, and in North America, where DST rules have changed over the years.
- **Exact coordinates of the birthplace:** the ascendant moves roughly one degree every four minutes, so location and time both matter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my Vedic chart differ from my Western chart?
Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, aligned with the fixed stars, while Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac tied to the seasons. The gap, the ayanamsha, is currently about 24 degrees, so most planets appear one sign earlier in your Vedic chart. Both are calculated correctly; they simply measure differently.

### How accurate does my birth time need to be?
As accurate as possible. A difference of a few minutes can change divisional charts and dasha dates, and about two hours can change your ascendant. A birth certificate or hospital record is best. If you are unsure, tell me the possible range and I will consider rectification.

### Can I use the chart you prepare with another astrologer?
Yes. The chart is calculated with standard classical parameters, Lahiri ayanamsha and whole-sign houses, so any Vedic astrologer can read it. Many clients keep it as a reliable reference for future readings, for Kundli Milan when marriage proposals arrive, and for choosing Muhurta dates for important events.
