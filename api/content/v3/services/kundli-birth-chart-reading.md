## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### The charts I prepare
- **D-1 Rashi chart (Lagna Kundli):** the foundation. The ascendant (Lagna), the twelve Bhavas and the placement, sign and dignity of all nine Grahas.
- **Chandra Lagna:** the chart read from your Moon sign (Rashi), which shows the mind and how events are experienced.
- **D-9 Navamsha:** the most important divisional chart. It confirms the true strength of each planet and shows marriage, dharma and inner maturity.
- **D-10 Dasamsha:** profession and public standing, used whenever career is part of your question.
- **Other Vargas as needed:** D-2 Hora for wealth, D-7 Saptamsha for children, D-12 for parents and D-24 for education.

### The timing systems
Prediction in Jyotish is always timing. I calculate your **Vimshottari dasha**, the 120-year cycle of planetary periods that begins from the Nakshatra lord of your Moon, down to the Antardasha and Pratyantardasha level. I then layer **Gochar**, the current transits of Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu, over your natal chart. Where it adds clarity I also use **Jaimini Chara dasha**, which reads time sign by sign.

### The classical texts behind the reading
My method rests on **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS)** for house meanings, planetary strength and dashas, and on **Phaladeepika** for yogas and practical results. From the **Jaimini Upadesa Sutras** I use the Chara Karakas: the Atmakaraka, your soul's significator, and the Amatyakaraka for career. **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** adds a second lens that reads how planets connect by sign, especially through the trines, and how Jupiter's transit activates those links.

## Key Life Questions & Chart Factors Analyzed

A full reading covers the whole chart, and I give extra time to the areas you ask about. These are the factors I weigh for the questions people bring most often:

- **Personality and life direction:** the Lagna and its lord, the Moon and its Nakshatra, the Sun, and the Atmakaraka in the Navamsha (the Karakamsha).
- **Career and money:** the 10th house and its lord, the 2nd and 11th houses of income and gains, the D-10, and Dhana or Raja yogas.
- **Relationships and marriage:** the 7th house, Venus and Jupiter, the Upapada Lagna and the D-9.
- **Health and vitality:** the Lagna lord's strength, the 6th and 8th houses and the luminaries. This is for awareness and prevention, never diagnosis.
- **Family, children and property:** the 4th, 5th and 9th houses and their significators.
- **Timing:** which dasha you are running now, what the next Antardasha brings, and how Saturn and Jupiter's transits support or test you.

I also check planetary dignity (exaltation, own sign, debilitation and any Neecha Bhanga cancellation), combustion, retrogression, Shadbala strength and the major yogas. A single difficult placement never decides a chart. I read the whole picture, then translate it into plain, practical language.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What do I need to send for a Kundli reading?
I need your date of birth, your exact time of birth (ideally from a birth certificate or hospital record) and the city and country where you were born. Please also send your questions, so that I can prepare your chart around what matters most to you before our session.

### What if I do not know my exact birth time?
A close estimate still allows a meaningful reading of the Moon sign, Nakshatra and dashas. For important decisions, I can attempt birth-time rectification by working backwards from the dates of major life events such as marriage, the birth of children or a career change, which narrows down your likely ascendant.

### Is a Vedic Kundli reading different from a Western horoscope?
Yes. Jyotish uses the sidereal zodiac, so most planets fall about one sign earlier than in Western astrology. It also relies on the Vimshottari dasha system for timing, the Nakshatras and divisional charts such as the Navamsha. This makes Vedic astrology especially strong for timing life events.
