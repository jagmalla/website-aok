> **Key takeaway:** Vedic medical astrology (Ayur Jyotish) maps the body through the Kalapurusha, the cosmic person whose head is Aries and feet are Pisces, and links planets with organs, doshas and the five elements. It highlights constitutional tendencies and timing for preventive care. It is never a diagnosis and never replaces a qualified medical professional.

## Classical Foundations in Jyotish

### The Kalapurusha
**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** describes the **Kalapurusha**, the "person of time", whose body is the zodiac. The signs follow the body from head to foot in the same order popular astrology uses:

- **Aries:** head and brain
- **Taurus:** face, eyes, mouth and throat
- **Gemini:** shoulders, arms and breath
- **Cancer:** chest and heart region
- **Leo:** upper abdomen and stomach area
- **Virgo:** intestines and digestion
- **Libra:** lower abdomen, kidneys and lower back
- **Scorpio:** reproductive and excretory organs
- **Sagittarius:** hips and thighs
- **Capricorn:** knees
- **Aquarius:** calves and ankles
- **Pisces:** feet

Classical texts assign the Kalapurusha's body to the houses as well: the 1st house is the head, the 2nd the face, and so on to the 12th, the feet. An astrologer therefore reads a sensitive area of the body from both the sign and the house.

### Planets, organs and doshas
The classical texts also link each planet with bodily systems and the three Ayurvedic **doshas**:

- **Sun:** vitality, heart, bones and eyes (Pitta).
- **Moon:** mind, fluids, blood and the breasts (Kapha and Vata).
- **Mars:** muscles, blood, energy and inflammation (Pitta).
- **Mercury:** nervous system, skin and speech (a balance of all three).
- **Jupiter:** liver, fat and growth (Kapha).
- **Venus:** reproductive system, kidneys and hormones (Kapha and Vata).
- **Saturn:** joints, teeth, nerves and chronic conditions (Vata).
- **Rahu and Ketu:** associated with sudden or hard-to-identify conditions in classical astrology.

### The five elements
The **Pancha Mahabhuta**, earth, water, fire, air and space, underlie both Ayurveda and Jyotish. Fire signs and planets relate to the fire element and Pitta, water signs to water and Kapha, air signs to air and Vata. A chart emphasising one element suggests where balance in diet and routine may help most.

## House, Planetary & Dasha Dynamics

### The houses of health
- **1st house and its lord:** overall constitution, vitality and resilience. A strong ascendant lord is a major protective factor.
- **6th house:** illness, the body's capacity to fight it, and daily habits.
- **8th house:** chronic matters, surgery and recovery, and longevity. Responsible astrologers do not make longevity predictions.
- **12th house:** hospitals, rest, sleep and recovery.

The **Moon** and the **Sun** are central to health readings: the Moon for the mind and fluids, the Sun for vitality.

### Dashas and transits as timing for care
Health themes tend to be emphasised during the **dasha of the 6th or 8th lord**, of planets placed in those houses, or when Saturn transits sensitive points, including Sade Sati. This is valuable for **prevention**: a period that suggests attention to digestion or stress is a reason to book a check-up, improve routines and rest more, not to worry.

### What medical astrology can and cannot do
It can:

- describe constitutional tendencies and the elements that need balance;
- suggest periods when extra care with health and routines is wise;
- support emotional wellbeing by encouraging calm and structure.

It cannot:

- diagnose any condition;
- predict death or the date of an illness;
- replace a doctor, tests or prescribed treatment.

In my consultations I never diagnose, and I always encourage clients to follow their doctor's advice. If a chart suggests a sensitive area, the recommendation is simply to give it attention, through professional check-ups and healthy habits.

### Classical illustration
A chart with the Sun weak in Libra and placed in the 6th house might suggest paying attention to vitality and stress, especially in the Sun's dasha. The practical response is daily morning sunlight, regular exercise, sleep and routine medical check-ups. It is a reminder to look after oneself, not a prediction.

## Conscious Karma & Practical Vedic Remedies

Health is where **Kriyamana karma**, present action, matters most. Most of these practices are also recommended by modern health guidance.

- **Dinacharya:** a steady daily routine, regular sleep, meals and movement.
- **Dhyana and Pranayama:** meditation and breathing practices such as Nadi Shodhana calm the nervous system and support every other remedy.
- **Swarodaya:** Shiva Swarodaya links the lunar left breath with rest and the solar right breath with activity. Awareness of the breath encourages balanced effort and recovery.
- **Pancha Mahabhuta balance:** cooling foods and calm for excess fire; warmth and grounding for excess air; movement and light foods for excess water and earth, always within medical advice.
- **Mantra:** the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra and the Surya mantra are traditionally recited for wellbeing and courage.
- **Dana:** support for hospitals, health charities or those who cannot afford care.
- **Gemstones:** evaluated carefully and never as a treatment for illness.

Used responsibly, medical astrology is a tool for awareness. It encourages you to care for your body in the periods and areas your chart highlights, together with, never instead of, qualified medical care.
