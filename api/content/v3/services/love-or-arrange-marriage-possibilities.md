## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Love and arranged-marriage indicators
- **The 5th house of romance and the 7th house of marriage:** a strong link between their lords, by conjunction, mutual aspect or exchange, is a classical indicator of a love marriage.
- **Venus:** the natural significator of love and partnership. Its strength and its connection to the 5th and 7th houses matter greatly.
- **Rahu:** often associated with unconventional or intercultural unions when linked to the 7th house or Venus.
- **The 9th house and Jupiter:** family blessings, tradition and an arranged match, especially when the 7th lord is connected to the 9th or 2nd houses.

### Jaimini and the Navamsha
From the **Jaimini Upadesa Sutras** I read the **Upapada Lagna** (the arudha of the 12th house), which describes the marriage and the spouse's family, and the **Darakaraka**, the planet of lowest degree, which signifies the spouse. The **D-9 Navamsha**, emphasised in **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**, confirms the strength of the 7th lord and of Venus. **Phaladeepika** describes the classical timing rules for marriage.

### Timing the marriage
Marriage typically happens in the dasha or Antardasha of the 7th lord, Venus, planets placed in the 7th, or the Darakaraka. It is supported by the widely used double-transit principle: Jupiter and Saturn both influencing the 7th house or its lord at the same time. **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** adds Jupiter's transit over natal Venus or its trines as a trigger.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can astrology tell the exact date of my marriage?
Astrology shows favourable windows rather than a fixed date. By combining the dasha of marriage significators with the double transit of Jupiter and Saturn, I can usually identify the most likely periods, often within a year or two, so you can approach those times with openness and confidence.

### What causes delay in marriage in a horoscope?
Common factors include Saturn aspecting or occupying the 7th house, a weak or afflicted 7th lord or Venus, Rahu or Ketu on the 1st–7th axis, and a demanding dasha. Delay is not denial: many of these combinations simply indicate a later, often more stable, marriage.

### Should I compare my chart with my partner's?
Yes. Once a specific person is in your life, a Kundli Milan comparing both charts gives far more reliable insight than reading one chart alone. It covers the Guna score, Mangal Dosha, both Navamshas and your shared dasha periods in the first years of marriage.
