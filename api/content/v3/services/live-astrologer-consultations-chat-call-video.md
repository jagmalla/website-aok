### Prepared before you call
Every live session starts with preparation. ### Choosing the right technique
- **Natal reading:** for life direction, patterns and the long-term picture.
- **Varshaphala (Tajika annual chart):** for the coming twelve months.
- **Prashna (horary):** for a single urgent question, even without a birth time.
- **Kundli Milan:** when a relationship or proposal is involved.
- **Muhurta:** when you need the best date for an event.
