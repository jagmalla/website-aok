> **Key takeaway:** Muhurta, Vedic electional astrology, chooses a time when the five limbs of the Panchang (Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga and Karana), the rising sign and the condition of Jupiter, Venus and the Moon support your goal. A personal Muhurta also checks Tara Bala, Chandra Bala and both partners' charts, giving important beginnings the best possible start.

## Classical Foundations in Jyotish

### The idea of Muhurta
Classical Jyotish has three main branches: **Siddhanta** (astronomy and mathematics), **Samhita** (mundane astrology and omens) and **Hora** (individual charts). Muhurta draws on all three. Works such as **Muhurta Chintamani** and **Muhurta Martanda**, and the electional chapters of earlier texts, set out rules for choosing times for marriage, travel, construction, education, business and spiritual ceremonies. The underlying idea is that the chart of a beginning shapes what follows, just as a birth chart shapes a life.

### The five limbs of the Panchang
- **Tithi:** the lunar day. The Rikta Tithis (4th, 9th and 14th) and Amavasya are generally avoided for auspicious beginnings.
- **Vara:** the weekday, each ruled by a planet. Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday are widely favoured for marriages and business.
- **Nakshatra:** the Moon's lunar mansion. For marriage, Rohini, Mrigashira, Magha, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Moola, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada and Revati are traditionally recommended.
- **Yoga:** a combination of the Sun's and Moon's longitudes. Vyatipata and Vaidhriti are among those avoided.
- **Karana:** half a Tithi. **Vishti (Bhadra)** is avoided for auspicious work.

### Periods to avoid
- **Eclipses:** solar and lunar eclipses, and the days around them.
- **Jupiter or Venus combust (Asta):** when either is too close to the Sun, marriages are traditionally postponed.
- **Chaturmas:** in many traditions the four months from Devshayani Ekadashi to Devutthana Ekadashi are avoided for weddings.
- **Kharmas or Malmas:** when the Sun transits Sagittarius or Pisces, and in an intercalary lunar month.
- **Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda and Gulika Kalam:** daily periods considered inauspicious for new starts.

## House, Planetary & Dasha Dynamics

### The rising sign of the Muhurta
A Muhurta is ultimately a chart. Its **ascendant** should be strong, ideally with benefics in the Kendras and Trikonas and malefics in the 3rd, 6th and 11th houses. The **8th house** should be free of planets, and for a marriage Muhurta the **7th house** should be clean and its lord well placed. Fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius) are often chosen for matters intended to last, such as a home or a business foundation.

### Marriage Muhurta
In addition to the Panchang, a marriage Muhurta considers:

- **Jupiter and Venus:** both should be strong and not combust. Jupiter's transit relative to the bride's Moon and the Sun's transit relative to the groom's Moon are traditionally checked.
- **Both partners' charts:** the Muhurta should not fall on the Janma Nakshatra of either partner or in an unfavourable Tara.
- **The running dashas:** a time when both partners run supportive periods is ideal.
- **Practicality:** families, venues and travel matter. A good astrologer offers several windows rather than a single date.

### Business Muhurta
For launches, registrations, signing agreements and opening a shop or office:

- A strong **10th house** for the business and a strong **11th house** for gains.
- A well-placed **Mercury** for trade and contracts and a strong **Jupiter** for growth.
- Favourable Nakshatras such as Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta, Chitra, Anuradha and Revati.
- The owner's **Tara Bala** and **Chandra Bala**, which check the day's Nakshatra and the Moon's transit against the owner's birth chart.

### Griha Pravesha and property
Entering a new home traditionally uses fixed or dual ascendants, a strong 4th house and an auspicious month, with the **Vastu** orientation also considered. Buying property or vehicles follows similar principles.

### Prashna when the birth time is unknown
When one partner's birth time is uncertain, the **Tajika Prashna** method can supplement Muhurta: a chart cast for the moment a sincere question is asked can confirm whether a proposed date is supportive.

## Conscious Karma & Practical Vedic Remedies

A good Muhurta is a favourable wind, not a guarantee. The intention, preparation and conduct of the people involved remain the decisive factors, which is the essence of **Kriyamana karma**.

- **Begin with prayer:** traditionally with Ganesha, the remover of obstacles.
- **Dana:** charity on the day of the beginning, such as food for those in need.
- **Mantra:** for a marriage, mantras for Jupiter and Venus; for business, for Mercury and Lakshmi.
- **Dhyana and Swarodaya:** a short meditation before signing or beginning, and attention to the breath: **Shiva Swarodaya** favours the lunar left breath for stable, auspicious undertakings such as entering a home, and the solar right breath for dynamic, competitive actions.
- **Pancha Mahabhuta:** a clean, bright, uncluttered space for the ceremony or opening.
- **When the ideal date is impossible:** choose the best available window, reduce its weak points, and strengthen the moment with prayer and charity. Muhurta is about doing the best that is possible, not about fear.

If you are planning a wedding, a launch or a move, a personalised Muhurta that considers your own chart gives you a clear shortlist and the confidence to begin well.
