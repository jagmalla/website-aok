## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can astrological consultations diagnose medical conditions or diseases?
No. An astrological reading is strictly for preventive wellness and self-awareness. It cannot and should never be used to diagnose, treat, or manage medical conditions. Always consult a licensed medical professional for health care.

### How does Ayurveda relate to Vedic astrology?
Jyotish and Ayurveda are sister Vedic sciences. Astrology maps the planetary indicators of Vata (Air/Space), Pitta (Fire/Water), and Kapha (Earth/Water), while Ayurveda applies lifestyle and nutrition to keep them balanced.

### Can astrological guidance help me manage chronic stress?
Yes. Identifying periods of intense planetary transit over your Moon or ascendant helps you schedule intentional downtime, adopt restorative breathing practices, and avoid burnout.
