ANALYSIS OF KARMA — Update (editable FAQs, meditation practices, mobile fixes)
==============================================================================

WHAT'S NEW IN THIS UPDATE
  • FAQs are now fully editable. Open Admin -> Visual Editor -> "Edit
    About Us" (or "Edit Contact Us"), scroll to the FAQ section, and you
    can:  + Add FAQ,  reorder with the up/down arrows, and remove any FAQ
    with the X button. Click the question/answer text to edit it.
  • Meditation -> "What we practise": you can now add, reorder and remove
    the practice cards. Open Admin -> Visual Editor -> "Edit Meditation",
    scroll to that section, and use  + Add practice  / the up-down arrows /
    X Remove. Click a card's title or text to edit it.
  • Cross-device compatibility checked and tightened on phones, tablets
    (iPad portrait & landscape) and desktop. Fixed a small horizontal-
    scroll issue on very narrow phones (360px) on the Contact page; all
    pages now fit cleanly with no sideways scrolling.

(Also includes the previous update: menu-bar editor, custom pages, and
 expanded social links — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Google Maps + add
 your own.)

HOW TO APPLY (1 file, nothing else changes)
  1. cPanel -> File Manager -> public_html (your web root).
  2. Upload the included index.html and choose "Overwrite".
     Only index.html changed. Do NOT touch api/, config.php, uploads/,
     or anything else. Your database content is untouched.
  3. Open your site and hard-refresh:  Ctrl+Shift+R  (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
  4. Go to /#admin, log in, then Visual Editor to use the new options.

All your existing content, photos and settings stay exactly as they are.
