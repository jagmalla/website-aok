## How Does Vedic Astrology Show a Love or Arranged Marriage?
Vedic astrology shows a love marriage when the 5th lord of romance and the 7th lord of commitment connect by aspect, conjunction or sign exchange (Parivartana). Involvement of the 9th and 2nd lords points to a family-supported arranged match, while Rahu or Venus on the 7th house suggests an unconventional union. The Navamsha (D-9) confirms long-term sustainability.

- **5th and 7th House Connections:** Classical Parashari rules state that mutual aspects, conjunctions, or sign exchanges (Parivartana) between the 5th lord (romance/attraction) and 7th lord (marital commitment) strongly indicate love marriage.
- **The Role of the 9th and 11th Houses:** Involvement of the 9th lord (family values, dharma) and 2nd lord (extended family) often signifies traditional family-supported arranged unions.
- **Rahu and Venus Influences:** When Rahu touches the marriage house or Venus, relationships are often unconventional, inter-cultural or self-chosen.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a chart show both love and arranged marriage combinations?
Yes. Many modern charts show a hybrid dynamic: an initial self-chosen love relationship that receives full parental blessing and traditional family integration.

### Does a delayed marriage mean a bad marriage?
No. In classical Jyotish, a Saturn delay in marriage often matures the person, leading to much more stable, durable, and conscious partnerships later in life.

### Can remedies help if marriage proposals keep falling through?
Yes. Strengthening the functional 7th lord, harmonizing Venus or Jupiter, and observing tailored mantra or fasting practices can help clear subtle karmic hurdles.
