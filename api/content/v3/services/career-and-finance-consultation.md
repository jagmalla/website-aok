## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### The houses of work and wealth
**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** assigns profession, status and action (Karma) to the **10th house**, accumulated wealth and family resources to the **2nd**, gains and income to the **11th**, and daily work, service and competition to the **6th**. These four houses (with the 10th and 6th forming part of the Upachaya houses that improve with effort) are where I begin. I study each house lord's sign, dignity and placement, and the planets that aspect them.

### The career significators
- **Sun:** authority, government and leadership.
- **Saturn:** discipline, long-term work, labour and service. It is the karaka of the 10th in Parashari astrology.
- **Mercury:** business, trade, communication, accounting and technology.
- **Jupiter:** teaching, advisory, law, finance and counselling.
- **Mars:** engineering, property, the police and armed forces, and surgery.
- **Venus:** the arts, design, hospitality and luxury goods.

### Divisional charts and Jaimini factors
The **D-10 Dasamsha** shows how your career unfolds in practice and confirms the planets that truly support your profession. The **D-2 Hora** and the 2nd and 11th lords show your financial temperament. From the **Jaimini Upadesa Sutras** I identify the **Amatyakaraka**, the planet of the highest degree after the Atmakaraka, which acts as the minister guiding your work. **Phaladeepika** lists the Dhana yogas (wealth combinations) and Raja yogas that I look for. **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** reads Saturn as the planet of profession and follows Jupiter's transit over natal Saturn and its trines to time career shifts.

## Key Life Questions & Chart Factors Analyzed

- **"Which field suits me?"** The 10th lord, the planets in and aspecting the 10th, the Amatyakaraka and the D-10 Lagna.
- **"Job or business?"** The 7th house of trade and partnerships against the 6th of service, and Mercury's and Saturn's strength.
- **"When will I change jobs or be promoted?"** Dashas of the 10th, 11th and 6th lords, and Saturn's and Jupiter's transits over the 10th house and its lord.
- **"Is this a good time to invest or expand?"** The 2nd, 5th and 11th lords, Dhana yogas and the current Antardasha.
- **"Why do I keep hitting obstacles?"** Afflictions to the 10th, Saturn's Sade Sati, and periods of the 8th or 12th lords.
- **Remedies:** steady, practical alignment through Saturn's discipline, Mercury's skill and Jupiter's ethics, supported by mantra and Dana. Never shortcuts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can astrology tell me which career is right for me?
Your chart shows natural aptitudes and the kinds of work where you are most likely to thrive, through the 10th house, the D-10 and the career significators. It points to directions, not a single job title, and works best alongside your own interests, education and practical opportunities.

### When is the best time to change jobs according to Vedic astrology?
Favourable windows usually come in dashas of the 10th, 11th or a well-placed Lagna lord, often when Jupiter transits the 10th or 11th house or aspects your 10th lord. I map these windows for the next few years so you can plan the move instead of reacting.

### Do you give investment or stock advice?
No. I do not recommend specific stocks, properties or financial products, and astrology is not a substitute for a licensed financial adviser. What I offer is timing and temperament: periods that favour expansion or caution, and how your chart handles risk, so you can make better-informed decisions.
