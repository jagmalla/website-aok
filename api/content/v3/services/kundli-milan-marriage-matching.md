## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Ashtakoota Guna Milan: the 36 points
The traditional matching system compares the Moon Nakshatras and Moon signs of the two partners across eight Kootas:

- **Varna (1 point):** spiritual temperament and mutual respect.
- **Vashya (2):** natural influence and attraction between the two.
- **Tara (3):** the auspiciousness of each birth star counted from the other.
- **Yoni (4):** physical and instinctive compatibility.
- **Graha Maitri (5):** friendship between the Moon-sign lords, the mental bond.
- **Gana (6):** Deva, Manushya or Rakshasa nature, the basic temperament.
- **Bhakoot (7):** the relationship between the Moon signs, linked to family welfare and prosperity.
- **Nadi (8):** the most heavily weighted Koota, traditionally linked to health and progeny.

Eighteen of 36 is the commonly accepted minimum. But Nadi and Bhakoot doshas have well-known classical exceptions (Parihara). For example, when both Moon signs share the same lord, or when the Rashi lords are friends, the dosha is considered cancelled. I always check these before calling anything a problem.

### Beyond the score: reading both charts
From **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** and **Phaladeepika** I assess each partner's 7th house and its lord, Venus as the natural significator of marriage, and Jupiter, especially in a woman's chart. The **D-9 Navamsha** is essential: it shows the quality and durability of married life. From **Jaimini** I use the **Upapada Lagna** and the **Darakaraka**, the spouse significator. I also compare the two charts directly: where each person's Moon, Venus and Lagna fall in the other's chart, the Vedic equivalent of synastry.

### Mangal Dosha with its cancellations
I check Mars from the Lagna, the Moon and Venus, and I apply the classical cancellations: Mars in its own sign or exaltation, the aspect or association of Jupiter, and both partners carrying a similar dosha. Most Manglik worries shrink considerably once these are applied honestly.

### Why the dashas after the wedding matter
Two charts can match well on paper and still meet a difficult stretch together if both partners enter testing dashas at the same time. That might be a period of a weak 7th lord, or Saturn transiting over both Moons (Sade Sati). So I map the next five to seven years of Vimshottari dasha for both of you, side by side. Seeing those chapters in advance lets a couple plan the wedding date, a move or children with open eyes, and support each other through any demanding period.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Guna Milan score of 18 out of 36 enough for marriage?
Eighteen is the traditional minimum, but the score alone is not enough. A high score can hide weak 7th houses, while a modest score with strong Navamsha charts and cancelled doshas can indicate a good marriage. I always read both full horoscopes before giving my view.

### Can Kundli Milan be done for a love marriage?
Yes, and it is often very reassuring. In a love marriage the decision is already in your hearts, so I focus on understanding each other's patterns, timing the wedding well and addressing any real dosha with remedies, rather than approving or rejecting the relationship.

### What if one partner is Manglik?
First I check whether the dosha is genuinely present from the Lagna, Moon and Venus, and whether classical cancellations apply. Many charts called Manglik are not, or the dosha is mild. Where it is real, matching with a similar chart or simple remedies are the classical answer.
