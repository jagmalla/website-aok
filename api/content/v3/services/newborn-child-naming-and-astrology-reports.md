## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Nakshatra and Pada: the source of the name
Each of the 27 Nakshatras is divided into four quarters (Padas), and each Pada has a traditional sound (Akshara). A child born in Ashwini, for example, has the syllables Chu, Che, Cho and La, one for each Pada. A child born in Rohini has O, Va, Vi and Vu. The Moon's exact position at birth gives the Nakshatra and Pada, and so the recommended first syllable. This is the classical basis of Namakarana, one of the sixteen Hindu samskaras.

### The child's chart
Following **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**, I prepare the **Lagna chart**, **Moon chart** and **Navamsha**, note the starting **Vimshottari dasha**, which comes from the birth Nakshatra, and read the child's temperament through the Lagna, the Moon and Mercury. Education and learning are read from the 4th and 5th houses and the **D-24**; health awareness from the Lagna lord and the luminaries. **Phaladeepika's** descriptions of planetary natures help translate this into simple guidance for parents.

### A gentle approach
Early childhood charts are read lightly. **Balarishta** (the classical childhood-health combinations) is discussed only with great care and alongside its cancellations. Health questions always belong with your paediatrician.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is a baby name chosen in Vedic astrology?
The Moon's Nakshatra and Pada at birth give a traditional starting syllable (Namakshar). Parents then choose a meaningful name beginning with that sound. I provide the syllables and examples in Punjabi, Hindi and English, and you are always free to choose a name that also reflects your family's heritage and preferences.

### Can we still use a name that does not match the Nakshatra?
Yes. Many families give an official name of their choice and keep a Nakshatra-based name for religious ceremonies and astrology. Tradition sees the Nakshatra name as an added blessing, not a requirement, and there is no harm in choosing a name your family loves.

### When should we book the naming consultation?
Ideally within the first days after birth, so you have the syllables before the Namakarana ceremony, traditionally held around the eleventh or twelfth day. If that time has passed, the Nakshatra-based name and the child's chart reading are equally valid at any age.
