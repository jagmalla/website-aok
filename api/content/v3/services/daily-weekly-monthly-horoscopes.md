## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Gochar from the Moon
Classical Jyotish reads transits primarily from the **Chandra Lagna**, your natal Moon sign, because the Moon is the mind that experiences events. **Phaladeepika** gives the well-known transit results of each planet from the Moon, along with the **Vedha** points that can obstruct a good transit. I also read transits from your Lagna for outer events, and I give special weight to the slow planets: Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu.

### Dasha first, transit second
A transit can only deliver what your **Vimshottari dasha** activates. So I begin with the Mahadasha and Antardasha you are running, then look at which transits touch those dasha lords. This is how a personal horoscope differs from a general forecast.

### Daily timing tools
For day-to-day planning I use **Tara Bala** (the strength of the day's Nakshatra counted from your birth star), **Chandra Bala** (the Moon's transit from your Moon sign), and the Panchanga elements Tithi and weekday. These help you choose good days for meetings, travel or new beginnings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is a personal horoscope more accurate than a general one?
A general horoscope uses only one sign, while a personal one reads transits from your exact Moon sign and ascendant and, crucially, your current dasha. Two people with the same Moon sign can have very different months because they are running different planetary periods. That is the difference a personal reading makes.

### Which sign should I use for Vedic horoscopes?
In Vedic astrology the Moon sign (Rashi) is the most important for transit predictions, followed by the ascendant. Because Jyotish uses the sidereal zodiac, your Vedic Moon sign may be one sign earlier than your Western sign. I calculate it exactly from your birth details.

### How often should I get a horoscope reading?
Many clients book once a year, often around their birthday, and add a short session when Saturn or Jupiter changes sign or a new Antardasha begins. Those are the moments when the themes of your life genuinely shift, so a review then is most useful.
