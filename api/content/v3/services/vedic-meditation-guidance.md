## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Reading the mind in the chart
In Jyotish the **Moon (Chandra)** is the mind (Manas). Its sign, Nakshatra, strength and associations show how you think, feel and react. Following **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**, I study the Moon together with the **Lagna lord** (your vitality and constitution), **Mercury** (the intellect), **Jupiter** (wisdom and faith), and **Ketu** and the **12th house**, which are linked to detachment, insight and Moksha. Rahu's placement often explains restlessness or overthinking. Your current dasha shows which of these forces is most active now.

### Breath science: Swarodaya Shastra
**Shiva Swarodaya** teaches that the breath alternates between the left nostril (Ida, lunar and cooling) and the right (Pingala, solar and heating), with Sushumna, the central channel, flowing at the junctions. The flow of the breath is linked to mental state and to the right time for different actions. I teach simple Swarodaya awareness alongside **Nadi Shodhana** (alternate-nostril breathing), Bhramari and gentle breath-counting. All of these are safe practices for beginners.

### Mantra and the five elements
Mantra is chosen to match your chart. That might be a planetary Beej mantra for the planet that needs support, the **Gayatri** for clarity, or a simple seed sound for beginners. I also draw on **Pancha Mahabhuta**, the five elements of earth, water, fire, air and space, and use simple elemental practices to balance the element your chart shows in excess or deficit. The philosophical grounding comes from the **six classical Darshanas**, particularly Yoga and Vedanta, but the practice itself stays simple and practical.

### Timing practice with the Moon
The tradition times practice by the lunar calendar. The quiet hours before sunrise (Brahma Muhurta) are considered the most supportive for meditation. **Purnima** (full Moon) and **Amavasya** (new Moon) are natural points to renew a commitment, and **Ekadashi**, the eleventh lunar day, has long been kept for fasting and inward practice. I show you how to use these rhythms gently, and how your Moon's Nakshatra suggests the hours and kind of practice that come most easily to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need any meditation experience?
No. Most people I guide are beginners. We start with simple breath awareness and a short mantra, practised for a few minutes a day, and build gradually. The practices are gentle and safe, and I adjust them to your health, schedule and comfort with sitting still.

### Why connect meditation to my birth chart?
Your chart shows how your mind naturally works through the Moon, Mercury, Rahu and your current dasha. Matching the practice to that temperament makes it easier to stay with: a restless mind may need breathwork first, while a heavy, low mood may need devotional mantra and movement.

### Is this meditation religious?
The practices come from the Vedic and yogic tradition, and mantras are in Sanskrit. But the guidance is about steadiness, clarity and self-awareness rather than religious belief. People of every background are welcome, and I always explain the meaning of a mantra before you use it.
