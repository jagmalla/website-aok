## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Functional benefics and malefics
**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** assigns each planet a role according to the houses it rules from your Lagna. The lords of the trines (1st, 5th and 9th) are natural supporters. Lords of the 6th, 8th and 12th houses are generally not strengthened, and planets that rule a mix of good and difficult houses need careful judgement. I confirm a planet's strength in the **D-9 Navamsha** and check whether it is running, or about to run, its **Vimshottari dasha**, because that is when a gem's effect is felt most.

### The nine Navaratna and their planets
- **Ruby (Manik):** Sun
- **Pearl (Moti):** Moon
- **Red Coral (Moonga):** Mars
- **Emerald (Panna):** Mercury
- **Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj):** Jupiter
- **Diamond (Heera):** Venus
- **Blue Sapphire (Neelam):** Saturn, which is always tested with great caution
- **Hessonite (Gomed):** Rahu
- **Cat's Eye (Lehsunia):** Ketu

### What I avoid
I do not recommend gems for the lords of difficult houses, for planets that are debilitated without cancellation, or for combinations of stones whose planets are mutual enemies. **Phaladeepika** reminds us that a planet's result depends on its dignity and placement, and gemstones follow the same logic.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I choose a gemstone by my Sun sign or Moon sign?
Not safely. In Vedic astrology the suitable stone depends on your ascendant (Lagna) and the houses each planet rules, not on your Sun or Moon sign alone. The same planet can be a strong supporter for one person and a functional malefic for another, so a personal chart check is essential.

### Do you sell gemstones?
No. I do not sell gemstones, earn commissions or recommend particular sellers. That keeps my advice independent. I tell you which stone, if any, suits your chart and what quality to look for, and you are free to buy it wherever you trust.

### Is Blue Sapphire (Neelam) dangerous?
Blue Sapphire is a powerful stone for Saturn and suits only some charts, mainly where Saturn is a functional benefic. It is traditionally worn on a trial basis first. I recommend it only when the chart clearly supports it, and I often suggest Saturn mantras and service instead.
