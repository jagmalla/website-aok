## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### The three kinds of karma
Vedic philosophy distinguishes **Sanchita** (accumulated karma), **Prarabdha** (the portion ripening in this life, shown by the birth chart and its dashas) and **Kriyamana** (the karma you create now through your actions). Astrology reads Prarabdha; remedies and conscious living work through Kriyamana. This framework, rooted in the **six classical Darshanas**, keeps every reading constructive rather than fatalistic.

### The karmic axis: Rahu and Ketu
**Ketu** shows what the soul has already mastered and may be ready to release; **Rahu** shows the new experience it is hungry for. Their houses and signs, and the planets they join, describe the central tension of a life. Their dashas, Rahu for 18 years and Ketu for 7 in the Vimshottari system, are often periods of major karmic turning.

### Other karmic indicators
- **Atmakaraka and Karakamsha (Jaimini Upadesa Sutras):** the soul's desire and its deepest lesson.
- **The 5th house (Purva Punya):** merit carried from the past, creativity and children.
- **The 9th house:** dharma, teachers and fortune.
- **The 12th house:** release, spiritual practice and Moksha.
- **The D-60 Shashtiamsha:** described in **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** as revealing past-life karma. It is used carefully, because it needs a very accurate birth time.
- **Saturn:** the karaka of karma itself, the teacher who rewards patience and honest effort.

**Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** adds its own lens: Ketu as the past and Rahu as the future, and the planets linked to them by sign showing which life areas carry the karmic story.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can astrology really show past lives?
Jyotish does not show detailed stories of past lives. It shows the karmic tendencies you carry, through Ketu, the 5th and 12th houses, the Atmakaraka and the D-60. I describe these as patterns and lessons, not as dramatic narratives, and always focus on what they mean for your life now.

### Is my karma fixed?
No. Only Prarabdha, the karma ripening now, is reflected in the chart. Kriyamana karma, what you do with your choices today, is always free. That is why Vedic remedies emphasise conscious action, meditation, service and charity: they change how the ripening karma is experienced and what you create next.

### What is the D-60 chart and do I need it?
The D-60 Shashtiamsha divides each sign into sixty parts and is traditionally linked to past karma. It changes every two minutes of birth time, so it is reliable only with a very accurate time. I use it when the time is certain, and otherwise rely on the nodes and karmic houses.
