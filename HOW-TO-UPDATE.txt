IMPORTANT: THIS UPDATE HAS MORE THAN ONE FILE
  Full step-by-step checklist: DEPLOY.md, "Part A - Upgrade the live site"
  and "Part C - Verify the upload". In short:

  1. Back up: download your current .htaccess and api/config.php, and use
     Admin -> Settings -> Backup -> Export content.
  2. Upload the files listed in DEPLOY.md (Part A2) into public_html/,
     public_html/assets/ and public_html/api/, with "Overwrite" ticked.
     This update contains no api/config.php, so your live database
     settings cannot be overwritten. Delete install.php if you still have it.
  3. Upload .htaccess LAST. If your old .htaccess had cPanel "php handler"
     lines, paste them into the marked block at the top of the new one first.
     (File Manager -> Settings -> "Show Hidden Files" to see .htaccess.)
  4. Log in at  https://www.analysisofkarma.com/admin  with your CURRENT
     password. It keeps working; the server secures it on that first login.
  5. Change your password: Admin -> Settings -> Security.
     (Recommended, because the old one was publicly readable before.)
  6. Check the Local & Business and Practitioner tabs, click Save changes.
  7. Open Admin -> SEO Health. The first group, "Server setup (after
     upload)", should be all green; each red item says how to fix it.
     If it lists pages without the classical sections, click "Apply Full
     32-Page Classical Content Upgrade". The score should then be 100.
  8. Hard-refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Put your old .htaccess back (or rename the new one to .htaccess-off).
  The site keeps working as before (admin at /#admin) while you check the
  steps above.

All your existing content, photos, leads and settings stay exactly as they are.
