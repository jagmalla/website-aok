> **Key takeaway:** In Jyotish a gemstone strengthens one planet, so it is chosen from your ascendant and the planet's functional role, not from your Sun sign alone. It helps only when that planet is a well-placed benefic for you. Gemstones support right action, Mantra and Dana; they never replace them, and the wrong stone can aggravate problems.

## Classical Foundations in Jyotish

### The Navaratna: nine stones for nine planets
Classical tradition, followed in texts such as the **Garuda Purana** and in later Jyotish practice, assigns one primary gemstone to each of the nine grahas:

- **Sun:** ruby (Manikya)
- **Moon:** natural pearl (Moti)
- **Mars:** red coral (Moonga)
- **Mercury:** emerald (Panna)
- **Jupiter:** yellow sapphire (Pukhraj)
- **Venus:** diamond (Heera), or white sapphire as a substitute
- **Saturn:** blue sapphire (Neelam)
- **Rahu:** hessonite garnet (Gomed)
- **Ketu:** cat's eye chrysoberyl (Lehsunia)

A gemstone is understood to **amplify** the planet it represents. That is why the key question is never "Which stone is lucky for my sign?" but "Which planet should be strengthened in my chart?"

### Why the ascendant matters more than the Sun sign
**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** explains that each planet becomes a functional benefic or malefic depending on the houses it rules from the ascendant. The lords of the Trikonas (1st, 5th and 9th) are generally beneficial. The lords of the 6th, 8th and 12th houses are generally not. A planet ruling both a Kendra and a Trikona becomes a **Yogakaraka**, especially helpful.

So the same stone can help one person and harm another. Blue sapphire strengthens Saturn, which is a Yogakaraka for Taurus and Libra ascendants, but rules the 8th house for Cancer and Leo ascendants. Red coral can support an Aries or Scorpio ascendant, where Mars rules the 1st, but may not suit a Gemini ascendant, for whom Mars rules the 6th and 11th.

## House, Planetary & Dasha Dynamics

### Life stones by ascendant: a starting point
Traditional practice often begins with three stones linked to the 1st, 5th and 9th lords:

- **Aries ascendant:** coral (Mars), with ruby (Sun, 5th) and yellow sapphire (Jupiter, 9th) considered.
- **Taurus:** diamond (Venus), with blue sapphire (Saturn, Yogakaraka) evaluated carefully.
- **Gemini:** emerald (Mercury), with diamond (Venus, 5th) considered.
- **Cancer:** pearl (Moon), with coral (Mars, Yogakaraka) considered.
- **Leo:** ruby (Sun), with coral (Mars, Yogakaraka) considered.
- **Virgo:** emerald (Mercury), with diamond (Venus, 9th) considered.
- **Libra:** diamond (Venus), with blue sapphire (Saturn, Yogakaraka) evaluated carefully.
- **Scorpio:** coral (Mars), with pearl (Moon, 9th) considered.
- **Sagittarius:** yellow sapphire (Jupiter), with ruby (Sun, 9th) considered.
- **Capricorn:** blue sapphire (Saturn), with diamond (Venus, Yogakaraka) considered.
- **Aquarius:** blue sapphire (Saturn), with diamond (Venus, Yogakaraka) considered.
- **Pisces:** yellow sapphire (Jupiter), with pearl (Moon, 5th) considered.

This list is only a starting point. The final choice depends on the planet's placement, strength and associations in your own chart.

### When a stone is not advisable
- The planet rules a Dusthana (6th, 8th or 12th) and has no redeeming role.
- The planet is placed in a Dusthana together with malefics, so strengthening it would strengthen the difficulty.
- The planet is involved in a damaging combination, for example with Rahu in a sensitive house.
- Two recommended stones belong to planets that are natural enemies and are considered unsuitable together, such as ruby and blue sapphire.

### Wealth and love
For **wealth**, Jyotish looks at the 2nd and 11th houses and their lords, Jupiter as the natural significator of wealth, and Dhana yogas between the 2nd, 5th, 9th and 11th lords. For **love and marriage**, it examines the 7th house and lord, Venus, Jupiter and the D-9 Navamsha. A stone for the lord of one of these houses can be helpful when that lord is a benefic for your ascendant, but no stone creates a promise the chart does not hold.

### The role of the dasha
Gemstones are most effective when the planet is running its **Mahadasha or Antardasha**, or about to. Wearing the stone of a well-placed dasha lord can help you make use of its period. Wearing the stone of a planet whose period is far away usually has little practical value.

### Traditional practice
Classical guidance covers weight, metal and the finger: yellow sapphire on the index finger in gold, blue sapphire on the middle finger often in silver or panchdhatu, ruby on the ring finger in gold or copper, emerald on the little finger. A stone is traditionally first worn on the planet's weekday during a favourable time. Good-quality, natural, untreated stones are preferred, and many people choose a **substitute (Uparatna)** such as amethyst for Saturn or citrine for Jupiter.
