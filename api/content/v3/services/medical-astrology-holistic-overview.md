## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Kalapurusha: the zodiac as the body
Classical Jyotish maps the twelve signs onto the cosmic person, the Kalapurusha, from head to feet: Aries the head, Taurus the face and throat, Gemini the shoulders, arms and lungs, Cancer the chest, Leo the heart and upper back, Virgo the digestion, Libra the lower back and kidneys, Scorpio the reproductive organs, Sagittarius the hips and thighs, Capricorn the knees, Aquarius the calves and circulation, and Pisces the feet. Afflictions to a sign and its house point to areas deserving care.

### Houses, planets and timing
From **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** and **Phaladeepika** I read:

- **The Lagna and its lord:** overall vitality and resilience.
- **The 6th house:** the tendency towards acute imbalance, and the capacity to recover.
- **The 8th house:** chronic or long-term patterns.
- **The 12th house:** rest, recovery and periods needing extra care.
- **The Sun and Moon:** constitutional strength and the mind-body connection.
- **The D-6 Shashthamsha:** traditionally used for health in the divisional scheme.

The **Vimshottari dasha** and Saturn's transits show when these patterns are more likely to be active, so you can plan rest, check-ups and healthier routines.

### Pancha Mahabhuta and the doshas
Each planet is linked to an element and to an Ayurvedic tendency: the Sun and Mars to Pitta (fire), Saturn to Vata (air), the Moon and Venus to Kapha and Vata, Jupiter to Kapha, and Mercury to a mix of all three. Reading these through **Pancha Mahabhuta**, the five elements, gives a picture of your constitution that can inform lifestyle choices made with qualified Ayurvedic or medical practitioners.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can medical astrology diagnose an illness?
No. Medical astrology cannot diagnose, predict a specific disease or replace a doctor. It shows tendencies, sensitive body areas and periods to be more careful, as a prompt for prevention and timely check-ups. For any symptom or condition, please consult a qualified medical professional.

### What is Kalapurusha in Vedic astrology?
Kalapurusha is the cosmic person whose body is mapped onto the twelve zodiac signs, from Aries at the head to Pisces at the feet. In medical astrology, a sign or house under affliction suggests the corresponding body area may deserve more attention and care.

### Which remedies do you suggest for health-related periods?
I suggest complementary practices only: rest and routine, Pranayama and meditation for the mind, mantra for the planets involved, charity, and balancing the five elements through lifestyle. These support, but never replace, medical treatment, and I always encourage you to follow your doctor's advice.
