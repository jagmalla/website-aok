ANALYSIS OF KARMA — Update (SEO, local SEO, AI search & security)
===================================================================

WHAT'S NEW IN THIS UPDATE
  • Google and AI assistants now see your real pages. Every page is built on
    the server with its own title, description, structured data and content,
    so ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google can read and cite it.
  • Every service has its own page:  /services/kundli-birth-chart-reading  etc.
    A new FAQ page lives at /faq. All your existing text is unchanged.
  • "Surrey, BC" is added to page titles in Google (not to your page text).
    You can switch this off in Admin -> Local & Business.
  • New admin tabs:
      Local & Business  - service area, hours, phone, Google Business links
      Practitioner      - your bio, training and expertise
      AI & GEO          - choose which AI assistants may read your site,
                          llms.txt, IndexNow
      Redirects         - review possible duplicate services / blog posts and
                          merge them yourself (nothing is merged automatically;
                          every merge can be undone), plus custom redirects
      SEO Health        - one score with a to-do list
  • Each service now has "Page address, SEO & redirect" settings; each blog
    post has Canonical URL and 301 redirect fields.
  • Faster: the logo is 13 KB instead of 2.4 MB, the website code is cached
    by browsers, and the 3D scene loads after the page (and is skipped on
    data-saver connections and low-memory phones).
  • Security: the admin password is now checked on the server and stored
    only as a secure hash. Your password and client leads are no longer
    readable in the public site data.

IMPORTANT: THIS UPDATE HAS MORE THAN ONE FILE
  Full step-by-step instructions: see DEPLOY.md, section
  "Upgrading the live site (SEO & security release)". In short:

  1. Back up: download your current .htaccess and api/config.php, and use
     Admin -> Settings -> Backup -> Export content.
  2. Upload everything EXCEPT api/config.php (keep your live one):
       index.html, render.php, sitemap.xml, robots.txt,
       the assets/ files, and the api/ files listed in DEPLOY.md.
  3. Upload .htaccess LAST. If your old .htaccess had cPanel "php handler"
     lines, paste them into the marked block at the top of the new one first.
     (File Manager -> Settings -> "Show Hidden Files" to see .htaccess.)
  4. Log in at  https://www.analysisofkarma.com/admin  with your CURRENT
     password. It keeps working; the server secures it on that first login.
  5. Change your password: Admin -> Settings -> Security.
     (Recommended, because the old one was publicly readable before.)
  6. Check the new tabs, add your phone number, click Save changes.
  7. Open Admin -> SEO Health. "Crawl files" should be all green.
  8. Hard-refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Put your old .htaccess back (or rename the new one to .htaccess-off).
  The site keeps working as before (admin at /#admin) while you check the
  steps above.

All your existing content, photos, leads and settings stay exactly as they are.
