## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### Parashari foundation
From **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** I read the twelve Bhavas, planetary dignity, Shadbala and Bhava Bala strength, and the complete set of divisional charts, the **Shodashavargas**. These include the D-9 Navamsha, D-10 Dasamsha, D-7 Saptamsha, D-12, D-20 Vimshamsha for spiritual practice, D-24 for learning, and the D-60 Shashtiamsha, the chart of deeper karma. **Phaladeepika** supplies the classical yogas: Pancha Mahapurusha, Raja, Dhana, Viparita Raja and Neecha Bhanga.

### Jaimini layer
The **Jaimini Upadesa Sutras** add a distinct way of seeing:

- **Chara Karakas:** Atmakaraka (the soul), Amatyakaraka (career), Darakaraka (spouse) and the others, set by planetary degree.
- **Karakamsha:** the Atmakaraka's sign in the Navamsha, which shows the soul's deepest inclinations.
- **Arudha Lagna:** how you are perceived. **Upapada:** marriage.
- **Argala:** how planets in the 2nd, 4th and 11th houses intervene on a house.
- **Chara dasha:** sign-based timing that complements Vimshottari.

### Tajika and Nadi
The **Tajika Neelakanthi** framework adds the annual Varshaphala chart and Prashna. **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** reads planetary relationships by sign, especially trines and the 2nd, 7th and 12th from each planet, and treats Jupiter's transit as the trigger that brings natal links to life.

### Philosophy behind the method
The **six classical Darshanas** and the doctrine of karma (Sanchita, Prarabdha and Kriyamana) frame the reading. The chart shows the karma ripening now; your conscious action shapes what comes next.
