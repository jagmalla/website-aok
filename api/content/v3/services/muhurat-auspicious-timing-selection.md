## Classical Vedic Methodology & Texts Used

### The five limbs of the Panchanga
- **Tithi:** the lunar day. Rikta tithis (the 4th, 9th and 14th) and Amavasya are generally avoided for new beginnings.
- **Vara:** the weekday and its planetary ruler, matched to the nature of the event.
- **Nakshatra:** the Moon's lunar mansion. Fixed (Sthira) Nakshatras such as Rohini and Uttara Phalguni suit permanent beginnings; movable ones suit travel.
- **Yoga:** the combined longitude of the Sun and Moon. Inauspicious yogas such as Vyatipata and Vaidhriti are avoided.
- **Karana:** the half-tithi. Vishti (Bhadra) is avoided for auspicious work.

### Lagna Shuddhi and personal strength
A good Panchanga alone is not enough. I choose a **rising sign (Lagna)** that is strong at the moment of the event, with benefics in the Kendras and the 8th house clear. Then I check **Tara Bala** (the day's Nakshatra counted from your birth star) and **Chandra Bala** (the transiting Moon from your Moon sign), so the date also suits you personally. For marriage I also confirm the bride's and groom's charts. I avoid **Rahu Kaal** and the classical eclipse and solar-transition periods.

### Sources
These rules follow the Muhurta tradition summarised in **Muhurta Chintamani**, read alongside **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** for planetary strength. I also use **Swarodaya Shastra**, which links the active breath (left or right nostril) to the right moment for different actions, as a simple personal check on the day itself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if no perfect Muhurta is available in my window?
A flawless Muhurta is rare, so classical practice is to choose the strongest available moment, prioritising a strong Lagna, a good Nakshatra and your personal Tara Bala, and to avoid only the serious defects. I always give you the best realistic option within your dates, with its strengths explained.

### Is Muhurta calculated for my local time in Canada?
Yes. A Muhurta must be calculated for the place where the event happens, because the Panchanga timings and the rising sign depend on location. For a wedding in Surrey or Vancouver I calculate in Pacific Time; for an event in India, for that city.

### Do I need everyone's birth details for a wedding Muhurta?
The bride's and groom's birth details are important, because their Moon signs and Nakshatras determine personal suitability through Tara Bala and Chandra Bala. Other family members are not required. If birth details are unavailable, I select the date from the Panchanga and Lagna alone.
