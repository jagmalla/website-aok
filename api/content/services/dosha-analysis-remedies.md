## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are doshas curses that ruin one's destiny?
Absolutely not. In classical Jyotish, a dosha simply signifies an area of concentrated karmic learning (Prarabdha Karma) requiring conscious attention, patience, and ethical alignment.

### Do I need to pay for expensive private rituals to fix a dosha?
No. We strongly advise against high-priced commercial rituals. The most potent classical remedies are mantra japa, breath awareness, voluntary charity, behavioral self-discipline, and elemental harmonization.

### Can a dosha resolve naturally over time?
Yes. Many doshas naturally soften as you mature past specific planetary maturity ages (e.g., Mars matures at 28, Rahu at 42, Ketu at 48) and as your Vimshottari Dasha changes.
